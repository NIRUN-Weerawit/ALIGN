Master–slave control

idle

Mirrors the master's outgoing joints and gripper at 20 Hz. Each joint maps proportionally between the measured master and slave ranges; out-of-range master commands and offset targets are capped to the slave's range without stopping the mirror. Gripper positions are mapped too, with at most 5 mm lead over slave feedback. Connect the arms on separate CAN buses. Stop holds the slave near its measured joint pose; hiding or closing this page stops the stream.
